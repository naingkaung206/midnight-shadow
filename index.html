What this app does

This is a private automation tool used solely by the owner of the "Midnight Shadow" YouTube channel to upload their own videos via the YouTube Data API.

Data we access

With your consent, the app uses the youtube.upload OAuth scope to upload videos to your own YouTube channel. No data is collected, stored, or shared with any third party.
